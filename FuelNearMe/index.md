# Fuel Near Me – Support

Fuel Near Me shows petrol stations near you, or near a town or postcode you search for, with current fuel prices from the UK Government Fuel Finder scheme.

## Contact

Questions, problems or suggestions: **paul.cozens.apps@icloud.com**

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and a short description of what happened. I aim to reply within a few days.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do the prices come from?**
From the UK Government Fuel Finder scheme, where retailers report their pump prices. Retailers must report changes within 30 minutes, and the app checks for new prices every 15 minutes.

**Is the price shown always what I'll pay?**
Not necessarily. Prices are reported by the retailers and can be out of date or wrong. Always check the price at the pump.

**The prices look old or the app says the server isn't ready.**
The government's data service occasionally has outages. When that happens the app keeps showing the last prices it received, and the "Prices fetched" time on the About screen tells you how recent they are. Pull down on the list, or use "Check for new prices" on the About screen, to try again.

**A station is missing, closed, or shows the wrong details.**
Station details come from the same government scheme and I can't correct them. The retailer is responsible for their own listing.

**Why does the app need my location?**
To find stations near you. Your location is used only on your phone and is never sent to me. If you'd rather not share it, you can search for a town or postcode instead. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy) for details.
