# Fuel Near Me – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

Fuel Near Me does not collect, store or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## Location

If you allow it, the app uses your iPhone's location to show petrol stations near you. Your location is used only on your phone to work out which stations are close. It is never sent to me or to any server run by me. You can decline location access and search for a town or postcode instead, or change your choice at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## Searching for a place

When you search for a town or postcode, the text you type is sent to Apple's location services so that it can be turned into a map position. This is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I don't receive the search text.

## Price data

The app downloads fuel price data from a server I run on Cloudflare. The request contains no information about you or your location. As with any internet service, the hosting provider may process technical details such as your IP address to deliver the data and keep the service secure; I don't use these to identify you, and the app doesn't send anything else. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Information stored on your device

The app saves its last downloaded price data and your preferences (such as fuel type, sort order and search distance) on your phone only, so it can work quickly and show prices when you're offline. Deleting the app removes it.

## Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
